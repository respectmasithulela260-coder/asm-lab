Validation

Checked against a textbook case

The flow solver is a finite-volume SIMPLE solver with Rhie-Chow fluxes, a k-epsilon turbulence model by default and a log-law wall function.

For a straight tunnel the pressure drop is within about 10 percent of Colebrook-White, and the mass balance is exact. ASM Lab's own friction number is shown next to the CFD result so the two can be compared.

What you get

  • SIMPLE with Rhie-Chow fluxes
  • k-epsilon by default
  • Wall function at the wall
  • ASM Lab numbers beside the CFD
