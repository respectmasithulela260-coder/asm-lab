CFD

Bends, slopes and tapers

A tunnel can curve and slope at once, and it can open or close along its length. The mesh bends with it and keeps its wall layers.

After the solve, pressure recovery in a widening tunnel is shown against the ideal value, so you can see how much is lost.

What you get

  • Curved and sloping paths
  • Taper along the length
  • Pressure recovery against the ideal
