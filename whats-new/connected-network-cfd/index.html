Solve a whole tunnel network in 3D, not one tunnel at a time

The 3D stope solver used to take one tunnel at a time. It now builds a connected network: every selected tunnel keeps its own section and path, and where tunnels meet they are joined through a junction cell.

Air enters at an inlet with its own speed, splits at the junction and leaves through the outlets. Mass balances across the junction, which the tests check.

What you get

  • Several tunnels in one solve
  • A speed for each inlet
  • Mass is conserved at every junction
  • Straight, curved and tapered tunnels can be joined

To know

Preview: the junction is one lumped cell, so the loss it gives is an estimate. It does not resolve the vortices inside a corner. Calibrating it against a resolved junction is under way.
