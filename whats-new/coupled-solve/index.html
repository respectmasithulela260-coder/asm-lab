Solver

Heat and humidity along the route

The coupled solve tracks heat and moisture along every airway. Heat per kilogram of air is capped, the water content follows the cap, and the wet-bulb search is bounded so it always ends.

When a limit is reached, the warning names the airways involved.

What you get

  • Heat and moisture together
  • Bounded wet-bulb search
  • Warnings name the airways
