Drawing

Say where the node sits on the section

Where the node sits on a tunnel's section decides where the tunnel hangs in 3D. You choose centre of area, floor or roof as a drawing setting, and it is kept for each tunnel.

The same rule is used by ducts, fans, the exports and the hand-off to the 3D stope solver. Where two tunnels hang differently at a join, ASM Lab shows a clash warning.

What you get

  • Centre of area, floor or roof
  • Kept per tunnel
  • Used by ducts, fans and exports
  • Clash warning at mismatched joins
