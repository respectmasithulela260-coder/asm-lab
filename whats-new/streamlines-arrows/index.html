Viewer

Watch the air move

One viewer shows the solved flow in several ways. Streamlines are coloured by speed, a cross-section can be placed anywhere along the tunnel, and the wall can be painted with a result.

Arrows travel along the streamlines, so you see the direction and speed at a glance. Turbulence fields are available too.

What you get

  • Streamlines coloured by speed
  • Movable cross-section
  • Travelling arrows with speed and size controls
  • Turbulence fields
