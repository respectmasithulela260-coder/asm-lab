Drawing your first mine

In this chapter you draw a small mine from nothing: an intake shaft, a level with two drives, a return shaft with a main fan. Then you solve it and read the results. It takes about fifteen minutes.

Task: Start a new project

  1. Start ASM Lab. On the start screen, click New model.
  2. A new, empty collection is made and the 3D view shows the grid.

Task: Draw the intake shaft

  1. Click Home > Create > Add shaft.
  2. A shaft is drawn like a tunnel, with two clicks: click the top of the shaft, move the cursor down (a trail shows the shaft) and click its bottom.
  3. In the Inspect panel, check the shaft's diameter and friction factor.

Task: Draw the level

  1. Open the Drawing pen on the left side bar and set the Working elevation to the depth of the bottom of the shaft.
  2. Click Home > Create > Draw tunnel, or press A.
  3. Click the bottom of the shaft to start, then click each point along the drive. Each click adds a node; points snap to the grid and to existing nodes.
  4. Right-click to finish the drive.
  5. Draw the second drive in the same way, from the end of the first to where the return shaft will be.
  6. Select a drive and set its width, height and shape in the Inspect panel.

Task: Draw the return shaft and add the main fan

  1. Draw a second shaft from the end of the level up to the surface.
  2. Click Home > Create > Place fan and choose Main fan.
  3. Click the return shaft. An arrow shows the direction the fan blows; click the side the air goes to, towards the surface for an exhausting fan.
  4. In the Inspect panel, choose a fan from the library or enter its curve.

Task: Set the intake and exhaust

  1. Click Network > Intake and click the top node of the intake shaft.
  2. Click Network > Exhaust and click the top node of the return shaft.

Task: Check and solve

  1. Click Home > Simulate > Check. ASM Lab lists anything that would stop a solve, such as a loose end or a missing boundary. Fix each one.
  2. Press F5, or click Solve.
  3. The solver log reports Converged.

Task: Read the results

  1. In Display, set Colour by to Velocity. The legend shows the range.
  2. Click an airway. The Inspect panel shows its flow, pressure drop, velocity and velocity pressure under Simulation Results.
  3. Click the fan's airway to see the fan's operating point: flow, total and static pressure, air and shaft power and efficiency.
  4. Click the play button on the left of the 3D view to watch the air move.

Task: Save the mine

  1. Choose File > Save and name the project My first mine.

You have drawn, solved and saved a mine. The next chapters describe each part of ASM Lab in more detail.
