Settings

Open the settings with Settings at the top right, or File > Settings. The settings apply to the whole program; settings that belong to one model, such as the site's surface conditions, are saved in its project file.

SectionWhat you set
ThemeLight or dark appearance.
DisplayColours, labels, line widths and how results are shown in the 3D view.
GeothermalRock properties and the virgin rock temperature with depth, for the thermal solver; presets for common regions.
EngineeringEngineering choices for the solver, such as the air density basis and the solver tolerance.
RegulatoryThe limits results are checked against: minimum and maximum air velocities, wet-bulb and gas limits.
Import / ExportUnits and options used when reading and writing files.
Note: The limits under Regulatory are starting values. Set them to your mine's own standards and your country's regulations.
