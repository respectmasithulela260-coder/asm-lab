ASM Lab 1.45 documentation

ASM Lab is a mine ventilation program for designing, checking and simulating the ventilation of underground mines: airflow, temperature and humidity, gases, dust and fire. This manual takes a new user from what ASM Lab is and how it works, around the screen and through a first mine, to each of its modules. Every task is written as numbered steps to follow at the computer.

The chapters listed below cover each part as follows.

Introduction
What ASM Lab 1.45 is, what it simulates, the mining methods it suits, what it is for, how it works and calculates, the ways to build a model, and the conventions used in this manual.
The ASM Lab interface
The areas of the window: menus, the ribbon and its tabs, the left and right side bars, the 3D view and its keys, and the solver log and status bar.
File handling
The .asmlab project file, opening, importing DXF, spreadsheets and models, saving, exporting, backups, recovering after a crash and cleaning up a model.
Settings
The Settings window: theme, display, geothermal, engineering, regulatory limits and import and export options.
Drawing your first mine
Step by step from an empty project to a solved mine: an intake shaft, a level, a return shaft with a main fan, boundaries, checking, solving, reading the results and saving.
Worked examples: airflow, pressure and regulators
Course questions on pitot tubes, airways in parallel, regulators and booster fans, solved by hand and with the ASM Lab network solver, with the error against the memo.
Worked examples: fans
Course questions on main fan duty, the fan laws, density changes and evasés, solved by hand and with ASM Lab, with fan charts and the error against the memo.
Worked examples: psychrometry and heat
Course questions on mixing air streams, heat absorption capacity, auto-compression in a deep shaft and pump-chamber heat, solved with charts and with ASM Lab.
Worked examples: refrigeration and cooling
Course self-study problems on plant duty, positional efficiency, COP and heat rejection, solved by hand and with ASM Lab, with the error against the memo.
