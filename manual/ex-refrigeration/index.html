Worked examples: refrigeration and cooling

The questions come from the MINN3019A mine ventilation course (tutorials, class calculations, self-study problems and lecture slides). Each one is set out as the question, the hand solution as in the memo, and then the same question answered with ASM Lab, with the difference from the memo. Memo values read off psychrometric charts carry the chart's reading accuracy, about 0.3 kJ/kg on sigma heat and 0.2 °C on a temperature, so differences of that size are reading differences, not errors.

Plant duty, positional efficiency and condenser water¶

Refrigeration self-study problem 1.

An underground system: chilled water 35.1 l/s entering the evaporator at 17.2 °C and leaving at 8.9 °C; 30.4 m³/s of air through the cooling coils, in at 31.8/33.9 °C and out at 27.5/27.5 °C, at 110 kPa; condenser water in at 38.4 °C and out at 44.3 °C; actual compressor COP 3.8. Find the plant duty, the positional efficiency and the condenser water flow.

Hand solution¶

Plant duty = evaporator duty = 35.1 × 4.187 × (17.2 − 8.9) = 1 220 kW
From the 110 kPa chart: S_in = 99.3, S_out = 79.9 kJ/kg, v = 0.836 m³/kg, so m = 30.4 / 0.836 = 36.4 kg/s
Coil duty = 36.4 × (99.3 − 79.9) = 706.2 kW; positional efficiency = 706.2 / 1 220 = 57.9 %
Compressor work = 1 220 / 3.8 = 321 kW; condenser duty = 1 541 kW
Condenser water = 1 541 / (4.187 × (44.3 − 38.4)) = 62.4 l/s
Answer: 1 220 kW; 57.9 %; 62.4 l/s.

With ASM Lab¶

Task: The coil duty from the psychrometric calculator

  1. Tools > Calculators > Psychrometry at 110 kPa: 33.9/31.8 °C and 27.5/27.5 °C give the sigma heats and the specific volume.
  2. The evaporator and condenser are water-side heat balances (m c Δt), the same in any tool.

An underground cooling plant¶

Refrigeration self-study problem 4.

Pump power at the cooling coil outlet 60 kW; line losses 160 kW from the evaporator to the coils and 95 kW back; evaporator at 5.9 °C, condenser at 46.1 °C; 50.0 m³/s of air into the coils at 29.0/35.0 °C, out at 23.0/23.0 °C, at 105 kPa; condenser water pump 80 kW; compressor motor 90 % efficient with an input of 583 kW. Find the plant duty, positional efficiency, overall COP, heat rejected in the tower, Carnot COP and overall cycle efficiency.

Hand solution¶

From the 105 kPa chart: S_in = 89.1, S_out = 64.9 kJ/kg, v = 0.872 m³/kg, so m = 50 / 0.872 = 57.3 kg/s
Coil duty = 57.3 × (89.1 − 64.9) = 1 385 kW
Plant duty = 1 385 + 60 + 160 + 95 = 1 700 kW; positional efficiency = 1 385 / 1 700 = 81.5 %
Overall COP = 1 700 / 583 = 2.9
Heat rejected = 1 700 + 0.9 × 583 + 80 + 60 = 2 365 kW
Carnot COP = (273.15 + 5.9) / (46.1 − 5.9) = 6.9; overall cycle efficiency = 2.9 / 6.9 = 42 %
Answer: 1 700 kW; 81.5 %; 2.9; 2 365 kW; 6.9; 42 %.
