The ASM Lab interface

When a model is open, the ASM Lab window has these areas:

  1. Menus: File, Edit, Mine and View.
  2. Ribbon: the tabs Home, Design, Duct and Network, each with groups of buttons.
  3. Top bar: undo and redo, display modes, the 3D and 2D stope solvers, Downloads, Help, Report bug and Settings.
  4. Left side bar: Outliner, Drawing pen, Colour and Clip.
  5. 3D view: the mine, with the view tools down its left edge.
  6. Right side bar: Inspect, Draw, Network, Solve, Thermal, ASM Assistant and Script.
  7. Solver log and status bar: at the bottom of the window.

The ribbon¶

The ribbon holds the commands you use most, grouped by what they do. The Home tab is where you draw, edit and solve. This chapter introduces the ribbon; each button is described where it is used in the chapters that follow.

TabGroups and what they hold
HomeCreate (Draw tunnel, Curved tunnel, Add shaft, Place fan, Add regulator, Add wall, Refuge bay, Add cooler); Edit (Duplicate, Merge nodes, Delete, Add node, Remove node, Fillet, Extend); Simulate (Check, Fan curves, Report, Fire, Air use, Charts, Library); Solve; Navigate; Display (Colour by, Label by).
DesignCreate: Split (cut an airway at a point), Add stope, Add door / airlock.
DuctAuxiliary ventilation: ducts, duct fans, size changes and evasés, with the duct's own 3D and 2D views.
NetworkBoundaries (Intake, Exhaust), fans (Main fan, Booster, Auxiliary), topology, the network report and exports.
Note: Hide ribbon, at the right end of the ribbon, gives the 3D view more room; click a tab to bring the ribbon back.

Task: Show and hide the ribbon

  1. Click Hide ribbon at the right end of the ribbon. The ribbon closes and the 3D view grows.
  2. Click the Home tab to open the ribbon again.

The left side bar¶

PanelWhat it is for
OutlinerEverything in the project: files, block models and collections (levels). Show, hide, rename, lock or delete them, and choose which collection new drawing goes into.
Drawing penDrawing settings, such as the working elevation new points are drawn at.
ColourColours for drawings, layers and block models.
ClipSix cutting planes to slice into the model, for looking inside a level or an orebody.

The right side bar¶

PanelWhat it is for
InspectThe properties of what you selected: an airway's size, shape, friction factor and device, a node's position and boundary, and their results after a solve.
DrawDrawing by typing coordinates, and other drawing dialogues.
NetworkBoundaries and the network checks (loose ends, gaps, disconnected parts).
SolveSolver settings and the latest solve.
ThermalHeat sources and thermal settings for the selected airway or the whole model.
ASM AssistantThe chat assistant that can draw, solve and explain the model.
ScriptThe script editor and the list of script functions.

The 3D view¶

The 3D view shows the model. The tools down its left edge fit everything in the view, reset it, and switch the grid, labels, flow arrows and flow animation.

KeyView
7Top
1Front
3Right
0Isometric
.Frame everything
5Perspective or orthographic

Solver log and status bar¶

The status bar at the bottom says what ASM Lab is doing and which collection you are drawing in. The solver log above it reports each solve: whether it converged, and any warnings. Click Show to open the full log.
