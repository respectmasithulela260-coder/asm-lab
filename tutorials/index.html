Tutorials

Your first mine ventilation project, step by step. Open a step to read it.

Download the latest version from the release page and run the installer. Open ASM Lab. Projects you save are .asmlab files, so you can double-click one later to open it.

Use the drawing pen to lay out airways node to node. Set the section and where the node sits on it (centre of area, floor or roof). Curves and slopes follow what you draw.

Open the Library, pick an item and press Place; the Library closes and shows where to click. Fans take a curve or a fixed flow, and regulators set a resistance.

Press Solve. ASM Lab gives airflow, pressure, density and mass flow for every airway. If tunnels sit on different collections, the solve names the ones it left out and offers Link them.

Run Check to find overlapping tunnels and unmerged nodes, and merge them. The Charts tab shows where the air is used and where the heat comes from.

Click the tunnels you want in the 3D view, press the 3D stope button and Import. Mesh and solve, then view streamlines, a cross-section, the wall and travelling arrows.

Open the Script panel, pick a template and press Run. Use await solve() and result() to read the numbers, and writeCsv to save them.
