ASM Lab · mine ventilation software

Feel the air

ASM Lab is a desktop program that designs and solves mine ventilation networks: how much air each tunnel gets, how hot it gets, and how it moves in 3D.

What we do

We help mine engineers answer three questions: does every working place get enough air, will it stay cool enough, and where does the air really go?

≋

Solve the network

Draw tunnels, add fans, doors and regulators, and get airflow and pressure in every airway.

☀

Heat and humidity

Follow dry bulb, wet bulb and moisture along the route and see where the heat builds up.

◎

See it in 3D

Send the tunnels to the 3D stope solver and watch streamlines, sections and travelling arrows.

Script anything

Build and change whole networks with JavaScript, then solve and export the results.
